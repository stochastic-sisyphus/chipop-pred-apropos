## Methodology Notes

This report applies the Context Augmented Generation (CAG) framework to bridge
statistical analysis with lived community reality. The approach:

1. **Builds Rich Context**: Combines domain knowledge about Chicago neighborhoods
   with statistical outputs from the pipeline models.

2. **Interprets Reality**: Translates quantitative metrics into qualitative
   community impact assessments, addressing the "GDP growth vs felt recession" gap.

3. **Discovers Patterns**: Uses structured analysis to identify non-obvious
   relationships in urban development data.

4. **Generates Blueprints**: Suggests next-step investigations based on findings
   to support continuous discovery.
