The Spillover Effect

Development doesn't stay contained. It spreads.

When West Loop booms, Pilsen feels the pressure. When Lincoln Park prices out families, Logan Square absorbs the overflow.

"Development pressure often spreads along transit corridors and displaces population to adjacent neighborhoods."

The Reality Gap

This is what we mean by Context Augmented Generation.

Statistics tell you what. Context tells you so what.

A 15% income increase in Bronzeville means something different than in Lincoln Park. The same "growth score" can mean prosperity or displacement.

The gap between data and reality is where policy fails - or succeeds.
